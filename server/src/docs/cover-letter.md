# Cover Letter Generator Guide

Welcome to ResumeTitan's Cover Letter Generator! This comprehensive guide will help you create compelling, personalized cover letters that complement your resume and increase your chances of landing interviews.

---

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Access the Cover Letter Generator
1. **Log in** to your ResumeTitan account
2. Navigate to your **Dashboard**
3. Click **"Add New"** under the Cover Letter section

### Step 2: Choose Your Input Method
You have two options to help the AI tailor your cover letter:

**Option A: Manual Entry**
- Enter the job title and company name
- Paste the job description or key responsibilities
- Provide additional context about the role

**Option B: Job URL Import**
- Paste the direct URL of the job posting
- Let our AI extract and analyze the job requirements
- Automatically align your cover letter with the position

---

## Filling Out Your Cover Letter

The generator will prompt you to enter key details to help tailor your letter. Here's what you'll need:

### Heading Information
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- City, State

### Job Target
- Job Title
- Company Name
- Job Description or Job Posting URL

---

## AI-Powered Letter Generation

Once you've provided the necessary inputs, click **"Generate Cover Letter."**

The AI will create a full draft that includes:
- A personalized introduction
- Key highlights from your background
- Alignment with the job responsibilities
- A strong closing statement

---

## Editing & Enhancing with AI

After generation, you can **manually edit** the letter or use the **AI Assistant** to revise specific parts.

### Sample Prompts for AI Editing

**Rewording:**
- "Make this more formal"
- "Use a more enthusiastic tone"
- "Simplify the language"

**Expanding:**
- "Add a sentence about my leadership experience"
- "Include more detail about my tech skills"
- "Mention my internship at Google"

**Polishing:**
- "Fix grammar and formatting"
- "Make this sound more confident"
- "Tailor this for a non-profit role"

Highlight a sentence or paragraph and enter your prompt to fine-tune it using the AI assistant.

---

## Saving & Exporting

- Click **"Save"** to store your cover letter to your dashboard.
- Download as a **PDF** or **copy as plain text** for easy use in applications.

---

## Best Practices for Cover Letters

- **Customize for each job**: Tailor your letter to the specific position and company.
- **Keep it concise**: Aim for 3-5 paragraphs, under one page.
- **Show your passion**: Highlight your motivation and fit for the role.
- **Connect your experience**: Relate your background to the job requirements.
- **Proofread**: Check for grammar, spelling, and clarity.
- **Use the AI as a helper**: Let the AI jumpstart your draft, but make sure it reflects your voice and values.
