## Tips for Success

> **Pro Tip**: Use specific keywords from job postings to increase your chances of passing ATS systems.

### Resume Writing Best Practices

1. **Keep it concise**: Aim for 1-2 pages maximum
2. **Use action verbs**: Start bullet points with strong action words like "Developed," "Managed," "Increased"
3. **Quantify achievements**: Include numbers and metrics where possible (e.g., "Increased sales by 25%")
4. **Tailor for each job**: Customize your resume for each application using relevant keywords
5. **Focus on recent experience**: Prioritize your most recent and relevant work experience
6. **Use consistent formatting**: Maintain uniform spacing, fonts, and styling throughout

### Cover Letter Best Practices

1. **Keep it to one page**: Concise, focused content works best
2. **Address the hiring manager**: Personalize when possible
3. **Connect your experience**: Link your background to the job requirements
4. **Show enthusiasm**: Demonstrate genuine interest in the role and company
5. **Proofread carefully**: Ensure no typos or grammatical errors

### Interview Preparation Tips

1. **Research the company**: Understand their mission, values, and recent news
2. **Practice common questions**: Prepare thoughtful responses to typical interview questions
3. **Prepare questions to ask**: Have intelligent questions ready for the interviewer
4. **Dress appropriately**: Match the company's dress code and culture
5. **Follow up**: Send a thank-you email within 24 hours

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

#### Resume Mistakes
- **Outdated Information**: Using old contact details or expired certifications
- **Irrelevant Experience**: Including jobs that don't relate to your target position
- **Poor Formatting**: Inconsistent fonts, spacing, or styling throughout
- **Generic Content**: Using the same resume for every job application
- **Missing Keywords**: Not including industry-specific terms from job postings
- **Too Much Information**: Including personal details like age, marital status, or photos

#### Cover Letter Mistakes
- **Generic Content**: Using the same letter for multiple applications
- **Too Long**: Exceeding one page or including unnecessary details
- **Not Addressing Requirements**: Failing to connect your experience to the job description
- **Poor Structure**: Missing proper greeting, body, and closing sections
- **Typos and Errors**: Grammatical mistakes or spelling errors

#### Interview Mistakes
- **Lack of Preparation**: Not researching the company or role beforehand
- **Poor Body Language**: Slouching, avoiding eye contact, or fidgeting
- **Rambling Answers**: Not being concise and focused in responses
- **No Questions Prepared**: Not having intelligent questions to ask the interviewer
- **Negative Talk**: Speaking poorly about previous employers or colleagues
