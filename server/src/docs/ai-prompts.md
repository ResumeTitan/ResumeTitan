# AI Prompts Guide for Resume Building

Welcome to ResumeTitan's AI Prompts Guide! This comprehensive resource will help you master our AI tools to create compelling, professional resumes that stand out to employers and pass ATS systems.

## Understanding Our AI Tools

ResumeTitan offers two powerful AI tools to enhance your resume building experience:

### Write With AI Tool
**Purpose**: Generate new content from scratch or expand on brief descriptions
**Best for**: Creating bullet points, summaries, and sections when you have limited content
**When to use**: Starting fresh, expanding brief descriptions, brainstorming new content

### AI Assistant Tool  
**Purpose**: Improve, refine, and enhance existing content
**Best for**: Polishing bullet points, rewording content, adding impact, ATS optimization
**When to use**: You have existing content that needs improvement or enhancement

## Write With AI: Content Generation Prompts

The Write With AI tool excels at creating professional content from your brief descriptions. Here are proven prompt strategies and examples:

### Work Experience Bullet Points

**Strategy 1: Role + Impact Framework**
```
"Create 3-4 professional bullet points for a [job title] role where I [brief description of main responsibilities]. Focus on measurable achievements and use strong action verbs."
```

**Example Prompts:**

*For Entry-Level Service Jobs:*
```
"Create bullet points for a waiter/server role where I provided excellent customer service to 50+ customers daily, managed multiple tables simultaneously, handled cash and credit card transactions, collaborated with kitchen staff to ensure timely food delivery, maintained cleanliness standards, and received positive customer feedback. Include specific customer service achievements and teamwork examples."
```

*For Sales Roles:*
```
"Generate bullet points for a sales representative role where I managed a territory of 50+ clients, conducted product demonstrations, exceeded quarterly targets by 15%, developed new client relationships, analyzed market trends, and collaborated with marketing team on lead generation campaigns. Include specific metrics and achievements."
```

*For Project Management:*
```
"Create bullet points for a project manager role where I led cross-functional teams of 8-12 members, managed budgets up to $500K, delivered projects on time and under budget, coordinated with stakeholders across departments, implemented Agile methodologies, and mentored junior team members. Include team size, budget amounts, and project outcomes."
```

*For Customer Service:*
```
"Write bullet points for a customer service supervisor role where I managed a team of 10 representatives, resolved escalated customer issues, improved customer satisfaction scores from 78% to 92%, implemented new training programs, reduced average call handling time by 20%, and developed customer feedback collection processes. Include specific metrics and improvements."
```

**Strategy 2: Challenge + Solution + Result**
```
"Create bullet points for a [job title] position where I faced [specific challenge] and implemented [solution approach] to achieve [measurable results]."
```

**Example:**
```
"Generate bullet points for a marketing manager role where I faced declining social media engagement and implemented a new content strategy and influencer partnerships to increase engagement by 60% and grow followers by 200%."
```

### Skills Section Enhancement

**Technical Skills:**
```
"Create a comprehensive skills section for a [job title] with expertise in [primary technical areas]. Include proficiency levels and relevant tools/technologies."
```

**Soft Skills:**
```
"Generate a skills section highlighting soft skills for a [job title] role. Include leadership, communication, problem-solving, and other relevant interpersonal skills."
```

**Industry-Specific Skills:**
```
"Create a skills section for a [specific industry] professional with [X] years of experience. Include both technical and industry-specific skills relevant to [target role]."
```

### Education and Certifications

**Academic Achievements:**
```
"Create bullet points for my education section where I earned a [degree] in [field] from [university] with [GPA/honors]. Include relevant coursework, projects, or academic achievements."
```

**Professional Certifications:**
```
"Generate content for my certifications section including [certification names] from [issuing organizations]. Include dates earned and brief descriptions of what each certification demonstrates."
```

## AI Assistant: Content Enhancement Prompts

The AI Assistant tool is perfect for refining and improving existing content. Here are effective prompt strategies:

### Bullet Point Enhancement

**Adding Quantifiable Impact:**
```
"Add specific metrics and measurable impact to this bullet point: [paste bullet point]"
```

**Example:**
```
"Add specific metrics and measurable impact to this bullet point: Managed social media accounts for company"
```

**Result:**
```
"Managed social media accounts for company, increasing engagement by 45% and growing followers by 1,200+ through strategic content planning and community management"
```

**Improving Action Verbs:**
```
"Rewrite this bullet point using stronger action verbs and more professional language: [paste bullet point]"
```

**Example:**
```
"Rewrite this bullet point using stronger action verbs and more professional language: Did customer service for clients"
```

**Result:**
```
"Delivered exceptional customer service to 50+ daily clients, resolving complex issues and maintaining 95% satisfaction ratings"
```

**Adding Technical Details:**
```
"Enhance this bullet point by including specific tools, technologies, or methodologies used: [paste bullet point]"
```

**Example:**
```
"Enhance this bullet point by including specific tools, technologies, or methodologies used: Analyzed data to improve processes"
```

**Result:**
```
"Analyzed customer data using SQL, Tableau, and Python to identify process inefficiencies, resulting in 25% faster response times and 30% cost reduction"
```

### Content Optimization for Specific Roles

**ATS Optimization:**
```
"Optimize this content for ATS systems by including relevant keywords for [job title] positions: [paste content]"
```

**Industry-Specific Tailoring:**
```
"Tailor this content for [specific industry] roles by using industry-appropriate language and focusing on relevant achievements: [paste content]"
```

**Senior-Level Positioning:**
```
"Rewrite this content to demonstrate senior-level leadership and strategic thinking: [paste content]"
```

**Entry-Level Adaptation:**
```
"Adapt this content for entry-level positions by emphasizing learning, growth, and potential: [paste content]"
```

### Tone and Style Adjustments

**Professional Tone:**
```
"Make this content more professional and business-appropriate: [paste content]"
```

**Confident Tone:**
```
"Rewrite this with a more confident and assertive tone: [paste content]"
```

**Concise Writing:**

**Detailed Expansion:**
```
"Expand this bullet point with more specific details and context: [paste content]"
```

## Prompt Best Practices

### Do's ✅

**Be Specific:**
- Include job titles, industries, and years of experience
- Mention specific tools, technologies, or methodologies
- Provide context about company size, team size, or scope

**Include Metrics When Possible:**
- Sales numbers, percentages, dollar amounts
- Team sizes, project budgets, timeframes
- Performance improvements, efficiency gains

**Use Role-Appropriate Language:**
- Match the tone and terminology of your target industry
- Include relevant keywords for ATS systems
- Use industry-standard action verbs

**Provide Context:**
- Explain the situation or challenge you faced
- Describe your approach or methodology
- Highlight the impact or results achieved

### Don'ts ❌

**Avoid Vague Descriptions:**
- Don't say "helped with various tasks"
- Don't use generic terms like "assisted" or "supported" without context
- Don't omit specific achievements or metrics

**Don't Overuse AI:**
- Review and personalize all AI-generated content
- Ensure accuracy and truthfulness of all claims
- Maintain your unique voice and experience

**Avoid Generic Prompts:**
- Don't use overly broad prompts like "make this better"
- Don't forget to specify your target role or industry
- Don't omit important context or constraints

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### AI Not Understanding Context
**Problem**: AI generates generic content that doesn't match your experience
**Solution**: Provide more specific context, including:
- Company size and industry
- Team size and structure
- Specific tools or technologies used
- Actual metrics and achievements

### Content Too Generic
**Problem**: AI produces vague, non-specific bullet points
**Solution**: Use prompts that request:
- Specific metrics and numbers
- Named tools or technologies
- Quantifiable results
- Industry-specific terminology

### Content Too Long or Short
**Problem**: AI generates content that doesn't fit your resume format
**Solution**: Specify length requirements:
- "Create 2-3 concise bullet points"
- "Expand this into 3-4 detailed bullet points"
- "Summarize this in one impactful bullet point"

### Missing Industry Relevance
**Problem**: Content doesn't align with your target industry
**Solution**: Include industry context in prompts:
- Target job title and industry
- Relevant keywords and terminology
- Industry-specific achievements and metrics
