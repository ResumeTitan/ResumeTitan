# Getting Started with ResumeTitan

Welcome to ResumeTitan! This guide will help you get started with creating your perfect resume using our AI-powered platform.

## Quick Start

1. **Sign Up**: Create your account using the sign-up button
2. **Pick A Section**: Choose from one the features in our virtual career center; create a resume, cover letter, or interview prep
3. **Add Your Information**: Fill in your personal details, experience, skills, and job information
4. **AI Enhancement**: Let our AI optimize your content
5. **Download**: Export your results as PDF

## Tips for Success

> **Pro Tip**: Use specific keywords from job postings to increase your chances of passing ATS systems.

### Resume Writing Best Practices

1. **Keep it concise**: Aim for 1-2 pages maximum
2. **Use action verbs**: Start bullet points with strong action words
3. **Quantify achievements**: Include numbers and metrics where possible
4. **Tailor for each job**: Customize your resume for each application

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Using outdated contact information
- Including irrelevant work experience
- Poor formatting and inconsistent styling
- Typos and grammatical errors
