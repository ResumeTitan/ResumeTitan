# ResumeTitan User Guide - Cover Letter Generator

Our Cover Letter Generator helps you quickly craft personalized, professional cover letters with the power of AI. Whether you're applying for your first job or your next big career move, this tool streamlines the writing process and gives you a polished draft in minutes.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Log in to your account** on the website.
2. From your **Dashboard**, under **“My Cover Letters”**, click on **“Add New”**

---

## Filling Out Cover Letter Information

The generator will prompt you to enter key details to help tailor your letter. Here's what you'll need:

### 1. Heading Information

Start with your contact details that will appear at the top of the letter:

- Full Name  
- Email Address  
- Phone Number  
- City, State

### 2. Job Target

You have two ways to help the AI tailor your cover letter:

#### Option A: **Enter a Job Title & Job Description**

Provide:

- The position you're applying for
- The company you're applying at
- A copy-paste of the job description or main responsibilities  

#### Option B: **Paste a Job Posting URL**

Paste the direct URL of the job posting. The AI will extract relevant information and align the cover letter accordingly.

---

## AI-Powered Letter Generation

Once you've provided the necessary inputs, click **“Generate Cover Letter.”**

The AI will create a full draft that includes:

- A personalized introduction
- Key highlights from your background
- Alignment with the job responsibilities
- A strong closing statement

---

## Editing with the AI Assistant

After generation, you can **manually edit** the letter or use the **AI Assistant** to revise specific parts.

### Sample Prompts for AI Editing

#### Rewording
- "Make this more formal"
- "Use a more enthusiastic tone"
- "Simplify the language"

#### Expanding
- "Add a sentence about my leadership experience"
- "Include more detail about my tech skills"
- "Mention my internship at Google"

#### Polishing
- "Fix grammar and formatting"
- "Make this sound more confident"
- "Tailor this for a non-profit role"

You can highlight a sentence or paragraph and enter your prompt to fine-tune it using the AI assistant.

---

## Saving & Exporting

- Click **“Save”** to store your cover letter to your dashboard.
- You can download it as a **PDF** or **copy it as plain text**.

---

## Pro Tips

- Customize your letter for each job to maximize your chances.  
- Keep it under one page.  
- Use the AI to help write, but make sure it reflects your voice and values.  
- Highlight your passion, not just your qualifications.
